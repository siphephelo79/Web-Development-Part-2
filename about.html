Our History

This organisation is based in Umlazi and is run by the father of the family. Mr Sithole started the business in 2018 when they moved to Umlazi. His two sons now help him fix cars, making it more of a family business today. Everybody in the community trusts Mr Sithole's business because of his ability to fix even badly wrecked cars.

Mission Statement

Sithole's Car Repair Shop is committed to providing the Umlazi community with reliable, affordable, and high-quality vehicle repair services. As a family-run business, we combine hands-on expertise with honest, personal service to get our customers safely back on the road, no matter how badly damaged their vehicle is.

Vision Statement

To be the most trusted car repair shop in Umlazi and the surrounding townships, known for quality workmanship, fair pricing, and building lasting relationships with every customer we serve.
